How to read it

Methods

  • DF-1 cells were crosslinked with microbial transglutaminase (TG, 5 or 10 wt% of cell wet weight) in a mold, Φ10 × 2 mm. Additives: 0, 1, 5 or 10 wt% of cell wet weight.
  • Heating: in water at 95 °C for 1 min. Texture profile analysis (TPA): two compressions with a Φ6 mm flat plunger.
  • Points are means of experiments (n = 3 for most conditions); tap a point to see mean ± SD and n. The grey box is chicken tenderloin, mean ± SD.
  • Cell powder 20 wt% was measured in one preliminary experiment (n = 1) and has no SD. Hide it with its button to zoom the axes back to the main data.
  • Axes keep the same scale for heated and unheated samples, so you can see how heating moves each condition.
